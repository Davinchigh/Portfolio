Why this works

The hill walk is your cardio base — already daily, already consistent. Everything else builds on top of it.

Strength days create "afterburn" (EPOC) — after a real resistance or circuit session, your body keeps burning extra calories for hours to recover, including while you sleep. That's the honest version of "burning fat overnight" — earned in the 20 minutes before, not magic on its own.

Four weeks, gently progressive — each week asks slightly more than the last. Rest days are built in on purpose, not as an afterthought.

Every exercise has an optional form video and a guided timer. Use them if they help, skip them if you already know the move — the ⓘ shows a quick cue, the ▶ opens a form video.
